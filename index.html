FamilyCar Listing Guidelines

Please read these important rules before listing your car. They help keep FamilyCar a trusted, spam-free marketplace.

  1. One Car Every 6 Months

    One user can list only one car within a 6-month period.

  2. Only Your Own Car

    You can list only a vehicle owned by you or your immediate family member. Ownership verification may be required.

  3. RC Upload is Mandatory

    A valid Registration Certificate (RC) must be uploaded to verify the vehicle.

  4. Vehicle Number is Mandatory

    You must provide the correct registration number. It will be used to identify and verify the vehicle.

  5. One Car, One Listing

    The same vehicle cannot have multiple active listings on FamilyCar, even from different accounts.

  6. No Dealers or Commercial Sellers

    FamilyCar is designed for genuine individual owners. Dealers, brokers, agents, and commercial resellers are not allowed to list vehicles.

  7. Dealer Activity May Lead to Restriction

    Accounts showing dealer-like activity, repeated listings, multiple vehicles, suspicious addresses, or attempts to bypass listing limits may be reviewed or restricted.

  8. Do Not Create Multiple Accounts

    Creating additional accounts to bypass the 6-month listing limit is prohibited and may result in all associated accounts being suspended.

  9. Upload Real and Recent Photos

    Upload clear, recent photos of the actual car being sold. Images downloaded from the internet or taken from another listing are not allowed.

  10. Provide Accurate Information

    Mileage, ownership, model, year, condition, accident history, asking price, and other information should be accurate. Intentionally misleading information may result in listing removal.

  11. Listings Are Subject to Verification

    Submitting a listing does not guarantee immediate publication. FamilyCar may verify the vehicle, documents, seller information, and listing before making it live.

  12. Suspicious Listings May Require Additional Verification

    FamilyCar may request additional documents or information if unusual or suspicious activity is detected.

FamilyCar is for genuine owners, not dealers.

By continuing, you confirm that the vehicle information provided by you is genuine and that you understand FamilyCar's listing rules.
